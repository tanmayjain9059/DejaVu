CAMPUS LOST & FOUND · DATA NOTICE

Privacy & AI processing

LostLink handles report, claim and campus-custody information so that lost property can be matched, verified and returned. This page explains the application behaviour currently implemented. It is not a substitute for the college's approved privacy notice or legal review.

Information collected

  • Report details: item description, category, visible characteristics, campus location and dates.
  • Claim details: ownership evidence, selected evidence types and review history.
  • Contact and attribution details: college roll number and any phone/email supplied in a public report.
  • Operational records: notifications, office actions, audit events, custody and reward records.
  • Optional photographs, stored in private object storage and not served in public browse results.

AI and photographs

Photo upload and AI photo analysis are separate choices. A photograph can be stored for office review without granting AI-processing consent. Image-based CLIP matching and optional image-caption suggestions run only when the report's explicit AI-consent flag is true.

AI model inference runs in the LostLink backend. Model downloads are disabled by default. If the operator enables downloads, the deployment must pin an immutable model revision. Text matching can continue without image AI. AI suggestions are advisory and never establish ownership.

Do not upload identity cards, access credentials, passwords, PINs, one-time codes or unrelated personal documents.

Private case access and data copies

Use the private tracking link from your submission receipt. The link contains a case reference, roll-number attribution and a private secret. Anyone who obtains that link may be able to access the case, so keep it confidential.

After opening your case in Track, use Download my case data to export the data associated with that public report or claim. Use Request a privacy action to submit an access, correction or erasure request to the authorised campus office.

Authenticated application users can use /api/privacy/export for an application-data export and /api/privacy/requests to submit or review their own requests.

Erasure, retention and review

Erasure requests are not executed automatically. Authorised staff must check whether an active ownership dispute, custody record, fraud-prevention need, audit obligation or other lawful retention basis requires some information to be preserved. A completed request must include a recorded action and reason. Supabase Auth identity removal, where applicable, also requires the authorised Supabase administrator to complete the provider-side action.

Retention schedule: the college has not yet supplied an approved retention schedule to encode. LostLink therefore does not automatically purge closed reports, claims, audit records or backups on an invented timetable. The college owner must approve retention periods and backup deletion procedures before representing this prototype as institutionally compliant.

Who handles requests?

Requests submitted through LostLink are routed to authorised office staff. The office review queue records the request type, status, decision note and timestamps. For a case submitted without an authenticated account, use the private tracking link to verify control of the case before requesting a privacy action.
